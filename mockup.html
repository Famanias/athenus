Overview

Data science provides the foundation for AI; modern AI systems both depend on and influence data-science processes.

Both domains use extensive jargon, prompting the creation of "periodic tables" as learning aids (not official standards).

Data-Science Periodic Table (learning aid)

Rows (maturity levels)

  • Raw data – source-level information.
  • Prepared data – cleaned, encoded, and transformed.
  • Model data – inputs for clustering, aggregation, etc.
  • Validation & governance – audited, governed data.

Columns (functional groups)

Acquisition, Preparation, Generation, Evaluation.

Quantum addendum

(optional for quantum-enhanced workflows): quantum encoding, quantum metrics, etc.

AI Periodic Table (learning aid)

Groups (columns)

  • Reactive – agents that act on input.
  • Retrieval – embeddings, vector databases (memory).
