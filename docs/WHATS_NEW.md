# What's New in Athenus 🎉

Welcome to the latest update! We've been hard at work turning Athenus from a video
viewing tool into a complete learning companion. This update adds four brand-new
ways to learn from your lectures — and they all work together.

**The short version:** upload a lecture, and Athenus now automatically understands
the *big ideas* in it, then helps you remember them with flashcards, quizzes, and
progress tracking. Everything happens on your own computer, even without internet.

---

## 1. The Knowledge Blueprint 🗺️

**Think of it as a map of everything your lecture talks about.**

When you watch a lecture, Athenus reads the transcript and pulls out the key
concepts — the important terms and ideas — then draws them as connected dots on a
visual map. Arrows show how ideas are related (e.g., "you need to understand *Math
Basics* before you can understand *Neural Networks*").

**What you can do with it:**
- **Explore your map** — zoom in and out, drag it around, and click any dot to see
  a short explanation of that concept.
- **Search for ideas** — type a topic and jump straight to it on the map.
- **See how concepts connect** — ask for a "path" between two ideas to find out
  what you need to learn first.
- **Jump straight to the video** — every concept links back to the exact moment in
  the video where it's explained. One click, and you're there.

> 🌟 *Best for: seeing the "big picture" of a course before you dive deep.*

---

## 2. Flashcards (with Smart Spaced Repetition) 🎴

**Think of it as digital flashcards that learn when YOU forget things.**

Athenus automatically turns your lecture into flashcards — question-and-answer
cards, fill-in-the-blank cards, definition cards, and true/false cards. Flip a card
to reveal the answer, then rate how well you remembered it.

**The smart part:** Every time you rate a card, Athenus schedules when you should
see it again. Get it right easily → you'll see it again in a few days. Struggle
with it → it comes back sooner. This technique (called *spaced repetition*) is
scientifically proven to help you remember things long-term.

**What you can do with it:**
- **Flip through cards** with a satisfying 3D flip animation.
- **Rate your recall** — "Again," "Hard," "Good," or "Easy" — and let Athenus
  schedule your reviews.
- **Keep multiple versions** of a deck — regenerate cards and compare, without
  losing your old ones or your progress.
- **Export to Anki** — if you use the popular Anki app, you can take your Athenus
  flashcards with you (or bring back a CSV for spreadsheets).
- **Jump to the source** — every card links back to the part of the video it came
  from.

> 🌟 *Best for: memorizing key terms and definitions over time.*

---

## 3. Adaptive Quiz Studio 📝

**Think of it as a practice test that's actually fair.**

Athenus writes multiple-choice questions covering a good spread of your lecture's
topics — not just the one topic it happens to like. Answer a question and you
instantly see whether you're right, **why** (a clear explanation), and can jump to
the exact video moment for a refresher.

**What you can do with it:**
- **Take a timed quiz** and see your final score, including how long you took.
- **Review immediately** — right/wrong answers and explanations appear the moment
  you answer.
- **Diagnose weak spots** — the quiz records which concepts you missed, feeding
  into your revision plan (see below).
- **Retake and regenerate** — new quiz versions are created without erasing your
  past attempts, so you can track your improvement.

> 🌟 *Best for: checking how well you really understand a lecture.*

---

## 4. Learning Analytics Dashboard 📊

**Think of it as your personal report card and study coach in one.**

Athenus keeps track of everything you do — quizzes taken, flashcards reviewed,
concepts mastered — and shows it on a clean dashboard. No more guessing how much
you've studied or what to review next.

**What you can see:**
- **Your stats at a glance** — media uploaded, concepts mapped, cards reviewed,
  quiz scores, total study time, and your daily review streak 🔥.
- **Concept mastery** — a color-coded list showing how well you've learned each
  concept (green = got it, red = needs work).
- **Revision recommendations** — Athenus tells you exactly what to review next,
  prioritized by what you're weakest at.
- **Recent activity** — a timeline of everything you've done.

> 🌟 *Best for: seeing your progress and knowing exactly what to study today.*

---

## 5. One Connected Pipeline 🔄

The best part: it's all connected. Upload a lecture and Athenus follows the full
journey — **ingest → understand → memorize → test → master** — automatically.

- Watching + mapping builds your **Blueprint**.
- Your **Flashcards** come from the map's concepts.
- Your **Quizzes** test those same concepts.
- Your **Analytics** turn every review and quiz into a smarter revision plan.

You can watch the whole pipeline from the **Pipelines** page and jump to any stage
with one click.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of This Update

1. **Start with the Blueprint** — upload a lecture, open the map, and click through
   a few concepts to build context.
2. **Make flashcards a habit** — a few cards a day beats cramming. Trust the
   schedule; it's doing the thinking for you.
3. **Quiz yourself before moving on** — if you score below 60%, spend time on the
   concepts marked red in Analytics.
4. **Follow your revision list** — the Analytics page tells you what to review
   today; just click and go.
